About Me

Hi, I’m Ayoub — a Direct-Response Copywriter specializing in Landing Pages, Email Marketing, and Creative Ad Strategy for B2B & B2C brands.

My Core Philosophy

"Copywriting doesn’t create desire; it channels pre-existing desires."

An average writer in a starving market will always sell more than a genius writer in a saturated market. My job isn't to force people to buy — it's to align your offer with the exact stage of awareness your audience is in.

Why Work With Me?

Most copywriters just write words and leave. As someone with hands-on experience running Meta Ads from scratch and building ad creatives, I look at copy through a performance lens:

  • Lowering CPC & CPA
  • Maximizing ROI & Conversion Rates
  • Matching the exact awareness level of your prospect

I divide your target market into 4 stages of awareness:

  • Unaware: Completely oblivious to the problem.
  • Problem-Aware: Knows the pain, but not the solution.
  • Product-Aware: Knows what you sell, but is hesitating.
  • Most Aware: Ready to buy, just waiting for the right nudge.

By mapping the first line of your copy directly to where your reader stands, we capture attention and create a more relevant path from click to conversion.

My 4-Step High-Converting Copy Process

  1. Deep Audience Research
    "If you sell to everyone, you sell to nobody." I deep-dive into your target audience's desires, behaviors, pain points, and buying psychology.
  2. Angle & Data Audit
    Analyzing past copy and performance metrics to identify hidden weaknesses, common objections, and core product features to polish.
  3. Drafting 7 Distinct Angles
    I develop seven different copy variations during focused work sessions, exploring multiple ways to communicate the offer and connect with the right audience.
  4. Execution & Optimization
    Turning winning angles into landing pages, emails, and ad creatives designed to support measurable growth.
